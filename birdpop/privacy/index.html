BIRDPOP Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026. This policy covers the BIRDPOP Android game by Arksow.

Game data stays on your device

BIRDPOP stores level stars, earned worm coins, owned birds, your selected bird, and audio volume on your device. The game has no account, advertising, analytics, or in-app payments. It does not send your game progress or personal data to us or to other companies.

Permissions

BIRDPOP uses touch controls. It does not access your camera or microphone.

Storage and deletion

Progress remains on your device until you clear BIRDPOP's app storage or uninstall the app. The Android release excludes app data from backup and device transfer. Clearing storage permanently removes your progress and unlocks.
